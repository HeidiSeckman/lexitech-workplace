# 🏛️ LexiTech Pro Workspace — Unified Legal & Tax AI Command Center
### Cloud-Saved Matter Vault & 4-Engine Forensic Suite by Heidi Seckman

🌐 **LexiTech Studio Headquarters:** https://heidiseckman.github.io/LEXITECH/

## 🚀 Overview
**LexiTech Pro Workspace** unifies all of LexiTech's closed-book legal and tax AI engines inside a single account-based SaaS platform backed by **Supabase Authentication & PostgreSQL Cloud Storage**. Users create an account, organize client or personal case folders, run multi-engine forensic audits, and permanently save every report and redline on file.

## ⚡ Included Workspace Engines
1. **📄 Legal & Tax Document Researcher:** Closed-book contract extraction & 61MB+ IRS reference manual verification with mandatory tab and page citations.
2. **⚡ Dual-Corpus Tax-Trap Scanner:** Cross-examines private legal agreements directly against official IRS publications to flag tax collisions and synthesize renegotiation redlines.
3. **🌪️ Worst-Case Life Event Contract Simulator:** Stress-tests leases, employment offers, and partnership contracts against emergencies (job loss, medical crisis, partner default, force majeure) to draft pre-signing escape clauses.
4. **✉️ Negotiation Email & Counter-Proposal Drafter:** Transforms detected legal or tax traps into polite, ready-to-send negotiation emails with protective redline language attached.

## 🗄️ Cloud Architecture
- **Frontend & AI Orchestration:** Python, Streamlit, Google Gemini API (`google-genai`)
- **Auth & Persistent Matter Storage:** Supabase Auth + Row-Level-Secured PostgreSQL (`projects` & `reports` tables)
